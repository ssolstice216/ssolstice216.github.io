This Privacy Policy explicitly governs the mobile application Instant.now: Instant Camera (package name: com.s2.instant.camera), developed and operated by ssolstice ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparently disclosing how our application handles device permissions, user data, and photo processing.

1. Overview of App Functionality

Instant.now: Instant Camera is an analog photography simulator that emulates retro instant camera models, optical lens filters, skeuomorphic acoustics, haptic feedback, chemical photo development, and a local instant gallery on Android devices.

2. Permissions Requested & Data Usage

To deliver its core instant camera experience, the app requests the following Android permissions. All data processed through these permissions is strictly handled locally on your device:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Required solely to capture photos within the application when you tap the physical shutter button. Camera streams are processed locally in real-time to render viewfinder previews and apply optical lens filters.
  • Storage / Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to save your developed instant photos directly to your device's local gallery and display them inside the in-app Local Instant Gallery.
  • Vibration & Haptics (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used to provide multi-stage tactile vibration feedback during shutter ejection, dial turning, and film tray loading.
  • Accelerometer & Motion Sensors: Used locally to detect phone shaking, allowing users to accelerate the simulated chemical photo development process.

3. Local Data Processing & Storage Security

Your photos belong 100% to you.

  • No Cloud Uploads: Instant.now does NOT transmit, upload, or backup your captured photos or videos to any remote server or external cloud.
  • No Facial Recognition: We do not scan, collect, or analyze facial data or biometric information.
  • 100% On-Device Processing: Chemical development simulations, optical filter rendering, and local gallery management are executed entirely on your Android hardware.

4. Automatically Collected Technical Data

We do not collect personal identifiers like your name, address, or phone number. However, the app may collect minimal, non-personally identifiable diagnostic information through integrated SDKs:

  • Device model, CPU architecture, and Android OS version.
  • Anonymous crash logs and performance diagnostics (to identify and fix technical crashes).
  • In-app purchase status via Google Play Billing (if Pro film packs or camera models are unlocked).

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Instant.now integrates trusted third-party service providers compliant with Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing (For app distribution and in-app purchases)
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics (For diagnostic crash logging)

6. Children’s Privacy

Instant.now: Instant Camera does not knowingly collect or request personal data from children under the age of 13.
